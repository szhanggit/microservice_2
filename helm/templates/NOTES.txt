Once the AWS Load Balancer Controller has provisioned the ALB for the
Ingress, find its hostname with:
  kubectl get ingress -n {{ .Values.namespace }} frontend -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].hostname}'

If ExternalDNS has picked up the Ingress's hostname annotation, the app is
also reachable at:
  https://{{ .Values.ingress.domain }}
